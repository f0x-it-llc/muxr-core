How-to guides: zellij, herdr and coding agents on your phone

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Four guides cover reaching zellij, herdr and terminal coding agents from an iPhone, iPad or Android phone.

  • How to use zellij on iPhone and iPad Attach to a zellij session on a Linux or Mac machine from an iPhone or iPad with Muxr, zellij's web client or an SSH app.
  • How to use zellij on an Android phone or tablet Attach to a zellij session on a Linux or Mac machine from an Android phone or tablet with Muxr, zellij's web client or an SSH app.
  • How to use herdr from your phone or tablet Reach herdr sessions from a phone with the herdr project's own mobile app, with Muxr, or with a community client.
  • Run Claude Code or Codex from your phone with zellij A coding agent in a terminal keeps running in a zellij or herdr session, and a phone client that attaches lets you watch and type to it.
