Muxr vs Zelmux: two zellij clients compared

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Zelmux is a free zellij client for iPhone and iPad that also handles tmux and plain SSH, while Muxr also runs on Android, works with herdr and has read-only tokens. Zelmux suits iPhone and iPad users who want one free app for zellij, tmux and SSH; Muxr suits people who also use Android or herdr and are happy to run its server, muxrd.

The short answer

Pick Zelmux if you want a free zellij client on iPhone or iPad, or one app for zellij, tmux and plain SSH.

Pick Muxr if you also use an Android phone or tablet, run herdr, or want read-only tokens for a view-only phone.

How do Muxr and Zelmux connect?

Zelmux’s listing says it attaches to zellij sessions over HTTPS and WebSocket and supports self-signed TLS with certificate pinning. It does not say what has to run on the server.

Muxr talks to muxrd, a daemon you run on your own machine next to zellij or herdr, over gRPC protected by TLS. You pair a phone by scanning a QR code shown by muxrctl. The code carries host, port and token; for a self-signed server it also pins the certificate fingerprint, and for a CA-signed server the app uses system trust. No SSH is used between the app and the server.

Where is Zelmux the better choice?

  • It is free. Muxr’s free app covers one server, and more than one server needs Muxr Pro.
  • One app handles zellij, tmux and plain SSH. Muxr’s tmux support is coming soon, and Muxr is not a general SSH client.
  • Its App Store listing describes it as “optimized for Claude and Codex cli”.

Where is Muxr the better choice?

  • Android. Muxr runs on Android 10 and later phones and tablets. Zelmux is listed on the App Store, and Android is not stated.
  • herdr. Muxr works with the herdr 0.9.x server (0.9.0 to 0.9.5), switches herdr spaces and lists the coding agents herdr reports, most urgent first. herdr-core is not supported. Zelmux does not state herdr support.
  • Read-only tokens. A phone paired with a read-only token can navigate tabs, panes and spaces and scroll, but cannot type or change the session.
  • Tablets and source. Tablets get a persistent pane rail beside the terminal, and the app and server are source available under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0.
