Muxr vs Moshi: self-hosted daemon or SSH terminal

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Published by the makers of Muxr.

The biggest difference is how each app reaches your terminal: Moshi connects by SSH, Mosh or Eternal Terminal with nothing to install on the server, while Muxr talks to muxrd, a daemon you run yourself next to zellij or herdr. Moshi suits people who want tmux, push notifications, voice input or Apple Watch today, and Muxr suits people who want a self-hosted, source available zellij and herdr client with a one-time price.

Which one should I pick?

Pick Moshi if you use tmux, want push notifications, voice input or an Apple Watch app, or cannot or do not want to install anything on the server.

Pick Muxr if you run zellij or herdr and are happy to install a small daemon, want source available code under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0, or want to pay once instead of subscribing. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027.

Where is Moshi the better choice?

  • tmux: Moshi supports tmux today. Muxr’s tmux support is coming soon.
  • Push notifications and voice input: Moshi sends push notifications when agents need you and offers on-device voice dictation. Both are coming soon in Muxr.
  • Apple Watch: Moshi runs on Apple Watch and lets you approve agents from the Lock Screen and the watch.
  • No server install: Moshi connects straight to your host over SSH, Mosh or Eternal Terminal. Muxr needs muxrd running on the machine.

Where is Muxr the better choice?

  • Price model: Muxr is free with one server, and Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Moshi’s paid plans are monthly, yearly or a lifetime price.
  • Self-hosting and source: You run the server yourself, and the code is source available under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0. Moshi does not state its source code or licence.
  • No SSH between the app and the server: The app connects to muxrd over gRPC and TLS with a per-device token and QR pairing.
  • Tablet layout: Muxr has a persistent pane rail beside the terminal on tablets. The Moshi pages we checked do not state a tablet layout.

Can I use both?

Yes. Moshi needs nothing on the server, so it can sit beside muxrd on the same machine. You could keep Moshi for tmux sessions and use Muxr for zellij or herdr.
