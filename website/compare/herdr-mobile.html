Muxr vs the Herdr mobile app

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Published by the makers of Muxr.

The Herdr mobile app is made by the herdr project, is free and is the natural first choice if you run herdr-core. Muxr is a different route: it also attaches to zellij, and connects through a daemon you run over TLS with QR pairing instead of SSH.

Which one should I pick?

  • Pick the Herdr mobile app if you run herdr-core. It is free, made by the herdr project, and its page says it works with herdr-core.
  • Pick Muxr if you also use zellij, want QR pairing over TLS instead of SSH, or want read-only tokens and a persistent pane rail on a tablet. If you run herdr-core, Muxr will not work for you; use the Herdr app.

Where is the Herdr mobile app the better choice?

It is the better pick when you run herdr-core. It is free and made by the herdr project, and its page states it works with herdr-core. Muxr does not support herdr-core.

It connects over SSH to herdr-core, and its page says there is no Herdr account and no server of the project's in between. Its page also says "Herdr TUI isn't supported".

Where is Muxr the better choice?

Pick it if you use zellij as well as herdr, because muxrd detects whichever is installed. Pairing is a QR code from muxrctl over TLS rather than SSH keys, read-only tokens let someone watch a session without typing, and tablets get a persistent pane rail. Push notifications are coming soon.

Is Muxr the same kind of app?

Both are phone clients for herdr, but they sit differently. Muxr needs muxrd on your machine and does nothing without zellij or herdr. It does not integrate with any specific agent; it shows the terminal the agent runs in.
