Muxr vs Termius: zellij client or SSH manager

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Termius is a general SSH client for desktop and mobile that reaches any server; Muxr is a client built for zellij and herdr that needs a daemon on the server. Termius suits you if you manage many machines over SSH, and Muxr suits you if you mostly want native session, tab and pane controls for zellij or herdr on a phone or tablet.

Which one should I pick?

Pick Termius if you manage many servers over SSH, need desktop apps and sync, SFTP or port forwarding, or use tmux. It needs no daemon on your server.

Pick Muxr if you run zellij or herdr and want to browse and control their sessions, tabs and panes with native screens, for a one-time price instead of a monthly plan.

Where is Termius the better choice?

  • You reach many different machines over SSH, SFTP or Mosh. Termius lists those protocols on its termius.com/pricing page, and Muxr speaks none of them.
  • You want one app on macOS, Windows and Linux as well as iOS and Android (termius.com). Muxr's desktop apps are coming soon.
  • You cannot install a daemon on the server. Muxr does nothing without zellij or herdr and muxrd; Termius needs only an SSH server (our judgement).
  • You use tmux today. Muxr's tmux support is coming soon.

Where is Muxr the better choice?

  • You live in zellij or herdr and want sessions, tabs and panes as native screens instead of the ordinary terminal UI drawn at phone size.
  • You want to pay once. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027. Termius lists its plans per month; a one-time purchase is not stated on its pricing page.
  • You want no account, read-only tokens for sharing a view, and a pane rail on tablets.
  • You want to read the code: Muxr is source available under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0. Termius's source code is not stated.

Can I use both?

Yes, and many people do. Termius can open a shell on a machine and run zellij attach inside it, which is how you reach a server where muxrd is not installed. Muxr is the purpose-built client for the machines where you do run it. They do not share data, and neither needs the other.

Muxr by F0X IT LLC (muxr.app) is a zellij and herdr client and is unrelated to the npm package @trymuxr/cli at trymuxr.com.
