What is Muxr?

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Muxr is a mobile app for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets that attaches to remote zellij and herdr terminal-multiplexer sessions. It talks to muxrd, a daemon you run on your own machine next to zellij or herdr.

Who makes Muxr?

Muxr is published by F0X IT LLC. It is source available. The server, muxr-core, is under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0. The app, muxr-app, is under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

What does Muxr work with?

Muxr works with zellij. Muxr works with the herdr 0.9.x server (0.9.0 to 0.9.5). herdr-core is not supported. The server detects whichever of zellij and herdr is installed. Without one of them Muxr does nothing, because it is not a general SSH client.

  • Phones and tablets: iOS 15.0 and iPadOS 15.0 or later, and Android 10 or later.
  • Server: x86_64 Linux, aarch64 Linux and Apple Silicon macOS have pre-built binaries. Intel Macs build from source. Windows is not supported.
  • Not available yet: tmux support, push notifications, voice input and desktop apps for macOS, Linux and Windows Coming soon

How does Muxr connect?

The app talks to muxrd over gRPC protected by TLS, signed in with a per-device bearer token. No SSH is used between the app and the server. You pair a phone by scanning a QR code shown by muxrctl.

The server listens on 127.0.0.1:50051 by default. A tailnet such as Tailscale is the recommended way to reach it from a phone; a LAN address or a public domain also work. Terminal traffic goes from the phone to your own server and does not pass through any server run by F0X IT LLC. The quick start walks through the setup.

What does Muxr cost?

Muxr is free to download, and the free app is the full app with one server. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027.

Pro unlocks more than one server. There is no subscription, and the purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play. The muxrd server is free to use under its licence; you host it yourself.

Is Muxr related to trymuxr or @trymuxr/cli?

Muxr by F0X IT LLC (muxr.app) is a zellij and herdr client and is unrelated to the npm package @trymuxr/cli at trymuxr.com.

Where can I get Muxr?

Get the app from the App Store or Google Play, then install the server from the quick start. Questions go to support@muxr.app.
