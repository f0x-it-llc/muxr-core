Best herdr mobile clients for iPhone and Android (2026)

Last updated: 15 November 2026

If you run herdr-core, use the Herdr mobile app, which is free and made by the herdr project. If you also use zellij, Muxr is one of the clients to try; for Android from F-Droid, look at Sherpr.

How was this list made?

This list is published by the makers of Muxr. It covers the herdr phone clients we could verify from their own pages on 15 November 2026, and looks up the same fields for each. A field a client's own pages do not state is left out of its entry and shown as "Not stated" in the table. It is not a hands-on review and has no scores.

Herdr mobile app (made by the herdr project)

  • What it is: an app to see which agents need you, read what they did and answer them
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Android
  • Price: free
  • Licence: app not stated; herdr itself is open source under Apache 2.0
  • Connection: SSH to herdr-core on each machine; no Herdr account and no server of the project's in between
  • Which herdr: "Works with herdr-core, which comes with the Herdr app. Herdr TUI isn't supported."
  • Agent features: an inbox sorted by needs you, done, working, idle
  • Source: herdr.dev/mobile

Collie

  • What it is: a self-hosted web app to drive Claude Code, Pi, Codex and OpenCode in Herdr, tmux or zellij
  • Platforms: opened in the phone's browser
  • Licence: MIT
  • Connection: runs a process on the computer
  • Agent features: push when an agent pauses or finishes
  • Source: github.com/AltanS/collie

Heeler for herdr

  • What it is: a native iPhone and iPad app for herdr
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad
  • Price: free
  • Connection: SSH
  • Source: App Store listing

herdr-mobile-relay

  • What it is: "Approve and monitor Herdr agents remotely from your phone"
  • Platforms: an installable mobile web app
  • Licence: AGPL-3.0
  • Connection: a relay on each computer
  • Source: github.com/0cv/herdr-mobile-relay

herdrup

  • What it is: an iPhone and iPad app to answer coding agents
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad
  • Price: free
  • Licence: Apache-2.0
  • Which herdr: needs herdrup's own fork of herdr
  • Agent features: push through its relay
  • Source: github.com/themartianapp/herdrup

Moshi

  • What it is: a mobile terminal that connects by SSH, Mosh or Eternal Terminal to your own machine to supervise coding agents
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision, Android and a desktop app
  • Price: a free tier; Lifetime is $199 on its website and $249.00 on the App Store
  • Connection: straight to your host; no relay
  • Which herdr: herdr supported, with a workspace picker; paying unlocks auto-attach for tmux and herdr
  • Agent features: push notifications, approvals from the Lock Screen and Apple Watch, an agents board
  • Source: getmoshi.app

Muxr

  • What it is: an app that attaches to zellij and herdr sessions through muxrd, a daemon you run on your own machine
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets
  • Price: free with one server. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027.
  • Licence: source available, PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0 for the app and the server
  • Connection: gRPC over TLS with a per-device token, paired by QR code; no SSH
  • Which herdr: Muxr works with the herdr 0.9.x server (0.9.0 to 0.9.5). herdr-core is not supported.
  • Agent features: an agents list on herdr, most urgent first. Push notifications are Coming soon.

Sherpr

  • What it is: an Android app for herdr
  • Platforms: Android 7.0 or later, on F-Droid
  • Licence: GPL-3.0
  • Connection: SSH, with no service in between
  • Agent features: on-device notifications
  • Source: f-droid.org/packages/org.sherpr.app

Whip

  • What it is: "An agent-native SSH mobile client, designed specifically for the Herdr multiplexer"
  • Platforms: Android and iOS
  • Licence: AGPL-3.0
  • Connection: SSH
  • Agent features: opt-in push through a relay
  • Source: github.com/kosumic/whip

Which should I pick?

  • You run herdr-core: use the Herdr mobile app. It is free and made by the herdr project.
  • You want push notifications today: the clients whose pages record them are Collie, herdrup, Moshi, Sherpr (on-device) and Whip. For Muxr they are Coming soon.
  • Android from F-Droid: Sherpr.
  • You also use zellij (Collie and Moshi list it too), or want QR pairing over TLS instead of SSH: Muxr (herdr 0.9.x server, 0.9.0 to 0.9.5; herdr-core is not supported).

All facts are as published by each product on 15 November 2026 and may have changed; follow the source links.
