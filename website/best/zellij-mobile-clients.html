Best zellij clients for iPhone, iPad and Android (2026)

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The zellij options for a phone or tablet are the zellij web client, Muxr, Zelmux, Moshi, and a general SSH app running zellij attach. The short answer: with nothing to install, the web client; on Android or with herdr too, Muxr; for a free iPhone or iPad app that also does tmux and SSH, Zelmux; for tmux or push notifications today, Moshi.

How was this list made?

This page is published by F0X IT LLC, the makers of Muxr. It covers the zellij-capable phone options we could verify from the products’ own pages on 15 November 2026, with the same facts for each. It is not a hands-on review of the other apps. It ranks nothing and gives no ratings or scores.

What is the zellij web client?

Part of zellij: attach to existing sessions, or start new ones, from a browser. On phones it offers touch scrolling, on-screen keys, pane and tab pickers and single-pane fullscreen.

  • Platforms: a web browser, or installed as a web app on phones
  • Price: free, because it ships with zellij
  • How it connects: start zellij web; HTTPS is required unless it listens on 127.0.0.1, and login tokens are required
  • Suits: zellij with no app and no extra daemon, or occasional access; it does not attach to herdr
  • Source: zellij web client documentation

What is Muxr?

A mobile app that attaches to zellij and herdr sessions through muxrd, a daemon on your own machine, with native session, tab and pane controls and a tablet pane rail.

  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad (iOS 15.0 or later), Android 10 or later; desktop apps Coming soon
  • Price: free with one server. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027.
  • How it connects: gRPC over TLS to muxrd with a per-device token, paired by QR code
  • Suits: native controls on Android or iPhone, and herdr 0.9.x; tmux and push notifications Coming soon
  • Source: source available under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0: muxr-app and muxr-core

What is Zelmux?

“A mobile client for Zellij, Tmux and SSH”, “optimized for Claude and Codex cli”, in the words of its listing.

  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad, iOS 16.0 or later; Android: Not stated
  • Price: free
  • How it connects: HTTPS and WebSocket to zellij sessions, with self-signed TLS and certificate pinning
  • Suits: a free zellij client on iPhone or iPad, or one app for zellij, tmux and SSH
  • Source: App Store listing

What is Moshi?

“A terminal built for phones” for supervising coding agents on your own machine. Its zellij support has a session picker and shortcut panel; it also supports herdr and tmux.

  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision (iOS 18.0 or later), Android, desktop
  • Price: free tier; Pro by month, year or lifetime (lifetime $199 on its website, $249.00 on the App Store)
  • How it connects: SSH, Mosh or Eternal Terminal straight to your host; no relay, no server install required
  • Suits: tmux today, push notifications, voice input, Apple Watch, or no server install
  • Source: getmoshi.app, pricing, zellij docs, App Store

What about an SSH app with zellij attach?

Any SSH client running zellij attach: the ordinary zellij UI at the phone’s screen size, driven by keyboard shortcuts.

  • Platforms: Termius on iOS and Android, Blink Shell on iPhone and iPad, Termux and ConnectBot on Android
  • Price: depends on the app: Termux is free, and Termius has a free Starter plan
  • How it connects: SSH; nothing beyond an SSH server
  • Suits: you already live in an SSH app, or cannot install a daemon
  • Source: termius.com, blink.sh, termux.dev and ConnectBot

Which should I pick?

  • No install on the phone and no extra daemon: the zellij web client.
  • Android, herdr or a tablet pane rail: Muxr.
  • A free iPhone or iPad app for zellij, tmux and SSH: Zelmux.
  • tmux or push notifications today: Moshi, or an SSH app for tmux.
