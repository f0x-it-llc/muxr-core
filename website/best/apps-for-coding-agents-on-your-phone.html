Best apps to control coding agents from your phone (2026)

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The options fall into three kinds: the agent vendors' own remote features, agent-specific companion apps, and terminal clients that attach to the session. The right one depends on whether you need notifications, a real terminal, or more than one agent.

How was this list made?

This page is published by the makers of Muxr. It is built from each product's own pages on 15 November 2026, with the same facts collected for each, and it is not a hands-on review. Entries are alphabetical within each group and nothing is ranked. "Not stated" means that product's pages say nothing we could find.

Which agent vendors have their own remote feature?

Claude Code Remote Control

Anthropic's way to continue a local Claude Code session from the Claude iOS and Android apps or claude.ai/code. It needs a paid Claude plan, and traffic goes through the Anthropic API. In our judgement it is the better pick if you use Claude Code only and want nothing to self-host. Source: Anthropic documentation, 15 November 2026.

Codex remote connections

The ChatGPT app on iOS or Android connects to a Codex host through "a secure relay layer". Price is not stated. Source: OpenAI documentation, 15 November 2026.

Which companion apps exist for coding agents?

Collie

An MIT-licensed self-hosted PWA that runs a process on your computer and opens in the phone's browser. Price not stated. Source: Collie repository, 15 November 2026.

Cosyra

"A mobile cloud terminal for AI coding agents" on iPhone and Android. It is a hosted cloud machine, not your own computer, at $30 per month for its 8 GB plan. Source: cosyra.com, 15 November 2026.

Happy

A free, MIT-licensed mobile and desktop client for iOS and Android, paired by QR code and end-to-end encrypted. Source: Happy repository, 15 November 2026.

KittyLitter

A native iOS and Android client described as open source and always free. It connects over the local network, SSH or QR pairing. Source: kittylitter.app, 15 November 2026.

Tactic Remote

An iPhone and iPad app with a companion for macOS, Windows and Linux, over a direct or relay connection. Price is not stated. Source: tacticremote.com, 15 November 2026.

Which terminal clients attach to the session?

Moshi

A terminal for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision and Android that connects by SSH, Mosh or Eternal Terminal straight to your host. The free tier has no account. Lifetime is $199 on its website and $249.00 on its App Store listing. In our judgement it is the better pick if you need tmux today, push notifications, voice input or Apple Watch, or do not want to install anything on the server. Source: getmoshi.app/pricing, 15 November 2026.

Muxr

A terminal client for iOS 15.0 or later and Android 10 or later that attaches to zellij and herdr sessions through muxrd, a daemon you run on your own machine. Traffic goes from your phone to your own server. The free app is the full app with one server. Muxr Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase, 50% off ($9.99) until January 13, 2027. Source: What is Muxr?

Secure ShellFish

An SSH terminal for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision and Apple Watch, with no Android app. It is free with in-app purchases from $2.99 per month, and it sets up agent hooks for notifications. Source: App Store listing, 15 November 2026.

An SSH app running zellij or tmux

This is a method, not a product: any SSH client, such as Termius, Blink Shell, Termux or ConnectBot, running the multiplexer in an ordinary shell. In our judgement it is the better pick if you already live in an SSH app or cannot install a daemon on the server.

Which should I pick?

  • I use Claude Code only and want nothing to host: Claude Code Remote Control.
  • I need to be alerted when an agent is waiting: not Muxr yet. Push notifications are coming soon in Muxr. Moshi, Collie and Secure ShellFish describe notifications today.
  • I want the real terminal of a zellij or herdr session, over my own server: Muxr.
  • I need tmux or Apple Watch today: Moshi.
  • I cannot install anything on the server: Moshi, which records no server install required. In our judgement a general SSH app also needs only an SSH server on the machine.
